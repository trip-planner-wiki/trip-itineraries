# Osaka: Day-Trip Itineraries

**Stay dates:** May 24–30, 2026  
**City overview:** Osaka is Japan's food capital and a city of bold, loud, joyful energy. Famous for "kuidaore" (eat until you drop), it delivers outstanding street food, a buzzing nightlife, and a uniquely approachable culture. The otaku district of Den-Den Town, the world-class Universal Studios Japan, the food-mad Dotonbori canal, and the relaxed neighborhood izakayas all make Osaka deeply rewarding to explore.

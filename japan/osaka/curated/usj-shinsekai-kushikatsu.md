# Osaka Day Trip: Universal Studios Japan, Shinsekai & Kushikatsu Daruma

**City:** Osaka  
**Best for:** Theme park thrills, retro neighbourhood walks, kushikatsu dining  
**Approximate duration:** Full day (11–12 hours)  
**Estimated cost:** ¥13,000–¥25,000+ (USJ entry + transport + dinner)

---

## Overview

This day packs the best of Osaka's two very different faces into a single, satisfying arc. Start with a full morning and afternoon at Universal Studios Japan — one of Asia's finest theme parks, home to Harry Potter, Super Nintendo World, Minions, and more. As the park crowds thin in the evening, head south to Shinsekai, Osaka's wonderfully retro "New World" neighbourhood, all neon signs, Tsutenkaku Tower, and shogi-playing locals. Cap the night with dinner at Kushikatsu Daruma Sohonten, the legendary 1929 original that invented the deep-fried skewer style. The pace is relaxed: USJ fills the day, and Shinsekai + dinner wraps up the evening without any rush.

### Morning to Late Afternoon — Universal Studios Japan (09:00)

🌐 [usj.co.jp/web/en/us](https://www.usj.co.jp/web/en/us)

Universal Studios Japan opens at 09:00 and deserves a full eight hours. The park spans ten themed zones: Super Nintendo World (with its Power-Up Band wristbands and interactive Koopa challenges), The Wizarding World of Harry Potter, Jurassic World, Minions Park, Hollywood, and more. Arrive at opening to catch Super Nintendo World before queues build — the zone uses a separate timed-entry system. Consider an Express Pass for the most popular rides if you want to cover more ground without long waits.

**Budget for this stop:** ¥8,600–¥20,000+ (standard entry ¥8,600–¥10,900; Express Pass ¥4,800–¥10,800+)

> ⚠️ **Tickets must be purchased online in advance** — gate ticket sales ended in May 2025.

### Late Afternoon / Evening Stroll — Shinsekai (17:30)

[![Shinsekai retro neighbourhood, Osaka](https://cdn.osaka-info.jp/cache/page_translation_500/b54aa3cc-04bf-11e8-8e48-06326e701dd4.jpeg)](https://osaka-info.jp/en/spot/shinsekai "Shinsekai — Osaka Tourist Info")

**📍 Ebisu-Higashi, Naniwa Ward, Osaka** *(5 min walk from Shin-Imamiya Station)*  
🌐 [osaka-info.jp/en/spot/shinsekai](https://osaka-info.jp/en/spot/shinsekai)

Shinsekai ("New World") was built in 1912 as Osaka's answer to Paris and New York — complete with an Eiffel-inspired Tsutenkaku Tower at its centre. Today it's a gloriously time-warped neighbourhood of neon pachinko parlours, old-school billiard halls, fugu restaurants, and shogi clubs. Spend about two and a half hours wandering Jan Jan Yokocho (the covered arcade), browsing souvenir shops selling the Billiken good-luck idol, and watching locals go about their evening. The district is open around the clock — the liveliest window is from dusk onwards when the signs light up.

**Budget for this stop:** Free (walking); optional Tsutenkaku Tower observation deck ¥1,000)

### Dinner — Kushikatsu Daruma Shinsekai Sohonten (19:30)

[![Kushikatsu Daruma Shinsekai — deep-fried skewers](https://res.cloudinary.com/dbm1qiew0/image/upload/blog-images/2024/09/799_ext_04_0--4-.jpeg)](https://www.magical-trip.com/media/kushikatsu-daruma-from-shinsekai-most-popular-kushikatsu-restaurant-in-osaka "Kushikatsu Daruma — Magical Trip")

**📍 2-3-9 Ebisuhigashi, Naniwa Ward, Osaka** *(2 min walk from Tsutenkaku Tower)*  
🌐 [kushikatu-daruma.com](https://www.kushikatu-daruma.com)  
🕙 Open 11:00–22:30 (last order ~22:00)

The Sohonten (main flagship store) opened in 1929 and is considered the birthplace of Osaka kushikatsu. Each skewer — beef, pork, prawn, lotus root, cheese, and dozens more — is coated in a light panko batter and deep-fried to a golden crisp. Dip once into the communal tonkatsu sauce, then eat. The golden rule of kushikatsu: **absolutely no double-dipping**. A leisurely dinner with drinks runs around 75 minutes. Queues can form at the door; arriving around 19:30 on a weeknight usually means a short wait.

**Budget for this stop:** ¥2,000–¥4,000 per person (skewers ¥180–¥400 each, beer/highball ~¥500)

## Tips

- **Book USJ tickets online well in advance** — tickets sell out on busy days and gate sales no longer exist. Check the official site for Express Pass availability alongside your standard entry ticket.
- **No double-dipping at Daruma** — the communal sauce bowl is shared between all diners; dipping a half-eaten skewer is considered bad manners (and unhygienic). If you want more sauce, use the provided spoon.
- **Getting from USJ to Shinsekai:** Take the JR Yumesaki Line from Universal City Station to Nishi-Kujo, then transfer to the JR Loop Line (Osaka Kanjo-sen) towards Tennoji, and alight at Shin-Imamiya. Total journey ~35–40 minutes; IC card (Suica/ICOCA) accepted.
- **Shinsekai is walkable and compact** — everything worth seeing is within a 10–15 minute stroll of Tsutenkaku Tower. Kushikatsu Daruma Sohonten is literally two minutes from the tower on foot.
- **Comfortable shoes are essential** — USJ involves a full day on your feet across a large park; plan accordingly so the evening stroll through Shinsekai stays enjoyable.

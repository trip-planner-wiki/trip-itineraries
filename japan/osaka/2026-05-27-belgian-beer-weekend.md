The Osaka edition of the globally celebrated Belgian Beer Weekend — one of Osaka's most beloved late-May annual events. Around 60–100 Belgian beer varieties are on offer, from crisp Witbiers and complex Trappist ales to rare sour lambics and fruit beers, all accompanied by live stage music, Belgian food stalls, and a festive riverside atmosphere at Nakanoshima Park. Free entry with drinks purchased using tokens at the venue. Nakanoshima Island also hosts two world-class art museums within easy walking distance, making this the perfect day to pair culture with craft beer — explore the museums in the morning before the festival opens, then settle in for an evening of Belgian beer and live music under the Osaka skyline.

> **Date-specific:** May 27–31, 2026. The festival runs for five days — attend any day or multiple days. Weekdays (May 27–29) are less crowded.

## Itinerary

🗺️ [Get transit directions from your accommodation to Nakanoshima Museum of Art Osaka](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Nakanoshima+Museum+of+Art+Osaka,+2-1-26+Nakanoshima,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&travelmode=transit)

### Morning — Nakanoshima Museum of Art Osaka / NAKKA (10:00)

**📍 2-1-26 Nakanoshima, Kita-ku, Osaka 530-0005**

Osaka's newest and most talked-about art museum, opened in 2022 in a striking matte-black cube building on Nakanoshima Island. The permanent collection spans 6,000+ works of modern and contemporary art — strong in 20th-century Western masters and the Osaka avant-garde movement. Check the current special exhibitions at https://nakka-art.jp before visiting. Budget ¥430–¥2,200 entry + 2–3 hours.

🗺️ [Directions to National Museum of Art, Osaka (walk, ~8 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Nakanoshima+Museum+of+Art+Osaka,+Nakanoshima,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&destination=National+Museum+of+Art+Osaka,+4-2-55+Nakanoshima,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&travelmode=transit)

### Late Morning — National Museum of Art, Osaka (12:30)

**📍 4-2-55 Nakanoshima, Kita-ku, Osaka 530-0005**

Japan's underground contemporary art museum, entered beneath a dramatic above-ground steel sculpture. Houses 8,000+ works with particular strength in post-war Japanese and international contemporary art. On Saturday visits, the permanent collection is free. Budget ¥430–¥1,800 entry (free permanent collection on Saturdays) + 1–2 hours.

🗺️ [Directions to Tenjinbashisuji Shopping Street (subway Yotsubashi Line ~12 min or walk 25 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=National+Museum+of+Art+Osaka,+Nakanoshima,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&destination=Tenjinbashisuji+Shopping+Street,+Tenjinbashi,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&travelmode=transit)

### Lunch — Tenjinbashisuji Shopping Street (14:00)

**📍 Tenjinbashi, Kita-ku, Osaka 530-0041**

Japan's longest covered shopping street (2.6 km, 600+ shops) is a treasure trove of affordable Osaka street food. Grab lunch from one of the many family-run eateries — try *takoyaki*, *kushikatsu*, or a set lunch at a local *teishoku* diner. The covered arcade keeps you cool in the late-May heat. Budget ¥800–¥1,500 for lunch + browsing time.

🗺️ [Directions to Belgian Beer Weekend at Nakanoshima Park (subway or walk ~15 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Tenjinbashisuji+Shopping+Street,+Tenjinbashi,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&destination=Nakanoshima+Park,+Naka-noshima,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&travelmode=transit)

### Afternoon — Belgian Beer Weekend opens (16:00 on weekdays / 11:00 on weekends)

**📍 Nakanoshima Park, in front of Osaka City Central Public Hall, Kita-ku, Osaka**

Arrive as the festival opens to secure a good seat. Purchase the Starter Set (≈ ¥3,000: official glass + 13 tokens) at the entrance — it's better value than individual purchases. Explore the 60–100 Belgian varieties systematically:

- **Witbier** (wheat beer) — light, refreshing, good starting point
- **Trappist or Abbey ale** — complex, malt-forward, exceptional quality
- **Lambic or Gueuze** — wild-fermented sour ales unique to Belgium
- **Fruit beers** — cherry (Kriek) or raspberry (Framboise) for something unique

Knowledgeable staff at many booths can guide your choices. Belgian food stalls serve waffles, frites, mussels, and stews. Budget ¥3,000–¥6,000 for a full evening of drinks and food.

### Evening — Riverside Live Music (18:00)

The live stage comes alive in the evening. The Nakanoshima riverside at night — illuminated Osaka skyline reflected in the Okawa River — is one of the city's most romantic settings. Stay as late as the event runs.

## Tips

- Weekday visits (May 27–29) are significantly less crowded than the weekend
- Bring a light jacket — the Nakanoshima riverside can be cool in the evening
- Nakanoshima is accessible from Osaka/Umeda Station (10 min walk) or Higobashi Station (Yotsubashi Line)
- The Osaka City Central Public Hall (1918) next to the festival is a Meiji-era architectural gem worth a look
- On Saturday May 30, the permanent collection at the National Museum of Art is free — the best day to combine both museums
- Token booklets are better value than buying individual tokens; surplus tokens can be spent on food

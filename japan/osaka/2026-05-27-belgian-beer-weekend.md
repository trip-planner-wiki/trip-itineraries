**City:** Osaka

**Dates:** May 27–31, 2026  
**Entry:** Free (drinks purchased with tokens)  
**Info:** https://belgianbeerweekend.jp

---

## Overview

[![Belgian Beer Weekend Osaka 2026 — official event image](https://belgianbeerweekend.jp/meta.jpg)](https://belgianbeerweekend.jp)

The Osaka edition of the globally celebrated Belgian Beer Weekend — one of Osaka's most beloved late-May annual events. Around 60–100 Belgian beer varieties, live stage music, authentic Belgian food, and a festive riverside atmosphere at Nakanoshima Park. Free entry with drinks purchased using tokens bought at the venue. The combination of excellent beer, riverside scenery, live music, and a social crowd makes this one of the best outdoor events of the trip.

> **Date-specific:** May 27–31, 2026. Available for 5 days — attend any day or multiple days.

## Itinerary

🗺️ [Get transit directions from your accommodation to Nakanoshima Park, Osaka](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Nakanoshima+Park,+Naka-noshima,+Kita-ku,+Osaka&travelmode=transit)

### Afternoon — Nakanoshima Park (15:00)
**📍 Nakanoshima Park, Naka-noshima, Kita-ku, Osaka**

Arrive in the afternoon to find comfortable seating before evening crowds arrive. Purchase token booklets at the entrance. The Nakanoshima Park rose garden is beautiful in late May — take a walk through before settling in with a beer.

### Early Evening — Belgian Beer Exploration (16:00)
With 60–100 Belgian varieties available, explore systematically:
- Start with a classic **Witbier** (wheat beer) — light and refreshing
- Move to a **Trappist** or **Abbey** ale for depth and complexity
- Try a **Lambic** or **Gueuze** for something unique
Knowledgeable Belgian beer representatives staff many booths and can guide your choices.

### Evening — Live Music & Food (18:00)
The live stage comes alive in the evening with performances. Belgian food stalls serve waffles, fries, mussels, and stews. Budget ¥3,000–¥6,000 for a full evening of drinks and food.

### Night — Riverside Atmosphere (20:00)
The Nakanoshima riverside at night with the illuminated Osaka skyline is one of the city's most romantic settings. Stay as long as you like — the festival runs until late.

## Tips
- Weekday visits (May 27–28) are significantly less crowded than the weekend
- Bring a portable charger and a light jacket for the evening riverside chill
- Token booklets are better value than buying individual tokens
- Nakanoshima is accessible from Osaka/Umeda Station (10 min walk) or Higobashi Station (Yotsubashi Line)
- The K-Ballet Tokyo performance (May 26–27) is at the adjacent Festival Hall — combine on May 27 for a cultural afternoon + beer evening

# Odaiba: teamLab Digital Art & Tokyo Bay

**City:** Tokyo  
**Best for:** Digital art, immersive experiences, sightseeing, couples/friends  
**Approximate duration:** Full day (7–8 hours)  
**Estimated cost:** ¥4,000–¥8,000

---

## Overview

[![Evening view of Tokyo Bay from Odaiba with Rainbow Bridge and city skyline](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/4/48/Evening_Tokyo_Bay_from_Aquacity_Odaiba_2.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Evening_Tokyo_Bay_from_Aquacity_Odaiba_2.jpg)

Odaiba is Tokyo's ultramodern waterfront island in Tokyo Bay — a futuristic entertainment district filled with digital art, unique architecture, and sweeping views of the Rainbow Bridge and Tokyo skyline. This trip pairs two world-renowned teamLab immersive art experiences with a relaxed afternoon on the island. Advance ticket booking is **required** for both teamLab venues. Odaiba was constructed entirely on reclaimed land during Tokyo's 1980s economic boom and was originally planned as a self-contained high-tech city; today it hosts a striking mix of science museums, shopping centres, and experimental architecture including Fuji TV's distinctive steel sphere. teamLab, the Tokyo-based art collective behind the island's signature immersive exhibitions, uses custom software and projection mapping to create environments where visitors become part of living, breathing digital ecosystems. The combination of glowing cityscapes viewed from across the water and the sensory wonder of teamLab's installations makes Odaiba one of the most visually memorable days possible in Tokyo.

> **⚠️ Book in Advance:** Both teamLab venues sell out days/weeks ahead. Purchase online before your trip.

## Itinerary

### Morning — teamLab Planets Tokyo (10:00)
**📍 6-1-16 Toyosu, Koto-ku, Tokyo**

Start at teamLab Planets — a body-immersive art museum with 4 large water-based art spaces and 2 gardens. Visitors go barefoot and wade through ankle-deep water as they enter infinity rooms full of flowers and light. Highlights include the Floating Flower Garden (thousands of real orchids suspended at eye level) and the Crystal Universe infinity room. Allocate 1.5–2 hours. Tickets purchased online.

### Lunch — Toyosu / Odaiba area (12:00)
Travel to Odaiba (25 min by Yurikamome monorail from Shimbashi, or Rinkai Line). DiverCity Tokyo Plaza has a large food court with many options. The life-size Unicorn Gundam statue stands outside in the plaza — a great photo opportunity.

### Afternoon — teamLab Borderless Tokyo (14:00)
**📍 Azabudai Hills Garden Plaza B, B1F, 5-9 Toranomon, Minato-ku, Tokyo**

The newer and larger of the two teamLab experiences at Azabudai Hills. Artworks freely move between rooms in this "borderless" concept — works wander out of their rooms and mix together. Each visit is unique because the experience changes based on how visitors interact. Allocate 2–3 hours.

> **Note:** teamLab Borderless is located in Azabudai Hills (not on Odaiba island). You can visit it after Planets as a second stop, or reorder the day if more convenient.

### Late Afternoon — Odaiba Sightseeing (16:30)
Back on Odaiba, take a stroll along Odaiba Marine Park beach for views of Rainbow Bridge and Tokyo Tower. The replica Statue of Liberty makes a fun landmark photo. Fuji TV's globe-shaped building is just nearby. The entire waterfront is relaxing and photogenic, especially as evening approaches.

### Evening — Rainbow Bridge Views at Dusk (18:30)
As night falls, the Rainbow Bridge and Tokyo skyline light up beautifully. This is one of the most iconic views in Tokyo. Many restaurants and cafes in the Decks Tokyo Beach mall have bay-facing windows. A perfect, low-key way to end the day.

## Tips
- teamLab Planets and teamLab Borderless are at **different locations** — plan your routing carefully
- Wear comfortable clothes you don't mind getting slightly wet at teamLab Planets
- The Yurikamome monorail (automated) offers great elevated views of Tokyo Bay on the way to Odaiba
- Odaiba is about 20–30 minutes from central Tokyo by public transport
- Weekdays are significantly less crowded at teamLab venues

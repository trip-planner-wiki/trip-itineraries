# Tokyo Day Trip: Gundam, Digital Art & Ancient Temples

**City:** Tokyo  
**Best for:** Culture, digital art, anime/mecha, temples, sightseeing  
**Approximate duration:** Full day (approx. 07:00 – 20:00)  
**Estimated cost:** ¥5,000–¥8,000/person (excluding meals and transport)

---

## Overview

A jam-packed day trip through Tokyo's most iconic experiences — from a 628 AD Buddhist temple to a life-size mecha statue and mind-bending digital art. This itinerary blends traditional and futuristic Tokyo across three distinct neighborhoods: Asakusa, Azabudai Hills, and Odaiba.

> **⚠️ Booking required:** teamLab Borderless tickets sell out, especially on weekends. Book online well in advance.

---

## Itinerary

🗺️ [Get transit directions from your accommodation to Senso-ji Temple, Asakusa](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Senso-ji+Temple,+2-3-1+Asakusa,+Taito-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Morning — Senso-ji Temple, Asakusa (07:00)

[![Senso-ji Temple, Asakusa, Tokyo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/d1/Senso-ji_Temple_%40_Asakusa_%2814019477806%29.jpg/960px-Senso-ji_Temple_%40_Asakusa_%2814019477806%29.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Senso-ji_Temple_@_Asakusa_(14019477806).jpg "Senso-ji Temple, Asakusa, Tokyo")

**📍 2-3-1 Asakusa, Taito-ku, Tokyo 111-0032**  
**Admission:** Free  
**Website:** https://www.senso-ji.jp/english/

Tokyo's oldest and most visited temple, founded in 628 AD. Start early to beat the crowds and soak in the Edo-period atmosphere. Walk through the iconic **Kaminarimon Thunder Gate**, browse the **Nakamise-dori** traditional shopping street, and explore the grand main hall and five-storied pagoda. Allow 2 hours here.

- Try **omikuji** fortune slips (¥100) at the temple
- Pick up traditional snacks on Nakamise-dori: ningyo-yaki (filled cakes) and kaminari-okoshi (puffed rice candy)

🗺️ [Directions to teamLab Borderless at Azabudai Hills (Tokyo Metro Ginza Line → Hibiya Line, ~45 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Senso-ji+Temple,+2-3-1+Asakusa,+Taito-ku,+Tokyo&destination=teamLab+Borderless,+Azabudai+Hills,+5-9+Toranomon,+Minato-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

> Take the **Tokyo Metro Ginza Line** from Asakusa Station, transfer to the **Hibiya Line** at Ueno, and alight at **Kamiyacho Station** (8-min walk to teamLab). Alternatively, taxi (~25 min, ¥3,000–¥4,000).

### Late Morning — teamLab Borderless Tokyo, Azabudai Hills (10:00)

[![teamLab Borderless, Azabudai Hills, Tokyo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/0/07/TeamLab_Borderless_Azabudai_Hills.jpg/960px-TeamLab_Borderless_Azabudai_Hills.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:TeamLab_Borderless_Azabudai_Hills.jpg "teamLab Borderless, Azabudai Hills, Tokyo")

**📍 Azabudai Hills Garden Plaza B, B1F, 5-9 Toranomon, Minato-ku, Tokyo**  
**Admission:** ¥3,200–¥3,800/adult (dynamic pricing; buy online in advance)  
**Website:** https://www.teamlab.art/e/tokyo/

An immersive "borderless" digital art museum where artworks freely move between rooms without boundaries, creating an ever-evolving interconnected world. Reopened in 2024 at the brand-new Azabudai Hills complex, it was named one of TIME magazine's *World's Greatest Places 2024*. Allow 3 hours.

- Download the **teamLab app** before visiting — no captions are displayed inside
- Wear comfortable shoes; some areas require removing footwear
- Grab a matcha experience at the **EN TEA HOUSE** inside the museum

🗺️ [Directions to teamLab Borderless area for lunch (walk within Azabudai Hills)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=teamLab+Borderless,+Azabudai+Hills,+Minato-ku,+Tokyo&destination=Azabudai+Hills,+Minato-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=walking)

### Lunch — Azabudai Hills / Roppongi (13:00)

[![Azabudai Hills, Tokyo — luxury dining and retail complex](https://cdn.housingjapan.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/17112127/azabudaihillsHeroUpdate-1024x683.jpg)](https://housingjapan.com/blog/explore-activities-at-tokyos-new-azabudai-hills/ "Azabudai Hills, Tokyo")

**📍 Azabudai Hills, Minato-ku, or nearby Roppongi**

World-class dining options are available on-site at Azabudai Hills. For more variety, head to nearby **Roppongi** (5-min walk). Allow 1 hour.

🗺️ [Directions to DiverCity Tokyo Plaza, Odaiba (Hibiya Line to Shimbashi → Yurikamome to Daiba, ~50 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Azabudai+Hills,+Minato-ku,+Tokyo&destination=DiverCity+Tokyo+Plaza,+1-1-10+Aomi,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

> Take the **Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line** from Kamiyacho to **Shimbashi Station**, then board the **Yurikamome automated monorail** to **Daiba Station** (5-min walk to DiverCity). Total: ~50 min.  
> 💡 **Scenic alternative:** Take the **Tokyo Water Bus** from Asakusa Pier to Odaiba (~50 min) for a leisurely route on the water.

### Afternoon — Unicorn Gundam Statue & DiverCity Tokyo Plaza, Odaiba (15:00)

[![Life-sized Unicorn Gundam statue, DiverCity Tokyo Plaza, Odaiba](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/6/66/Life-Sized_Unicorn_Gundam_Statue.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Life-Sized_Unicorn_Gundam_Statue.jpg "Life-sized Unicorn Gundam statue, DiverCity Tokyo Plaza, Odaiba")

**📍 DiverCity Tokyo Plaza, 1-1-10 Aomi, Koto-ku, Tokyo 135-0064**  
**Admission:** Free (statue & plaza)  
**Website:** https://mitsui-shopping-park.com/divercity-tokyo/

The most iconic Gundam statue in Tokyo — a life-size 19.7m RX-0 Unicorn Gundam that physically **transforms** between Unicorn Mode and Destroy Mode during scheduled daily shows (~6× per day at 11:00, 13:00, 15:00, 17:00, 19:00, 21:00). LED illumination and sound effects make the evening shows spectacular. Allow 2.5 hours.

- Visit **THE GUNDAM BASE TOKYO** (7th floor of DiverCity) for exclusive model kits, dioramas, and merchandise
- After the 17:00 show, stroll to **Odaiba Marine Park** (5-min walk) for Rainbow Bridge sunset views

🗺️ [Directions from DiverCity Tokyo Plaza to your accommodation](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=DiverCity+Tokyo+Plaza,+1-1-10+Aomi,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Optional Evening Extension (17:30–19:00+)
Stay in Odaiba for dinner with Rainbow Bridge views and catch the stunning **19:00 LED Transformation Show** after dark — the glowing psycho-frame effects are most impressive at night.

## Bonus POIs

The three main stops fill a full day, but if you adjust timing, consider adding:

| POI | Location | Why Add It |
|-----|----------|------------|
| **Akihabara Electric Town** | 15 min from Asakusa (JR) | Anime, manga, retro games — thematically on-brand with the Gundam visit. Add before teamLab (09:00–10:00) if you start Senso-ji at 07:00. |
| **Roppongi Hills / Mori Art Museum** | 5-min walk from teamLab | World-class contemporary art on the 53rd floor + observation deck. Open until 22:00. Excellent pre- or post-teamLab addition. |
| **Odaiba Marine Park** | 5-min walk from Gundam statue | Free outdoor beachfront park with a replica Statue of Liberty and the best Rainbow Bridge views. Zero extra travel needed. |

## Tips
- **Book teamLab Borderless tickets online well in advance** — slots sell out, especially on weekends
- Arrive at Senso-ji before 08:00 for a peaceful experience; it gets very crowded by mid-morning
- Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at any station for seamless train/bus/monorail travel
- The **Yurikamome Line** (Shimbashi → Odaiba) offers great elevated views of Tokyo Bay
- Check the Gundam **transformation show schedule** on the DiverCity website before visiting — the 19:00 show after dark is the most spectacular

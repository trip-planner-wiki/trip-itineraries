# Tokyo Day Trip: Art, Anime & Markets

**City:** Tokyo  
**Best for:** Digital art, anime/mecha, seafood, art supplies, waterfront  
**Approximate duration:** Full day (08:30–17:00, ~8.5 hours)  
**Estimated cost:** ¥5,500–¥9,000/person (teamLab tickets + food + browsing)

---

## Overview

This curated day trip sweeps across Tokyo's eastern and southern waterfront districts, threading together four very different experiences: a world-record immersive art museum, Tokyo's most beloved seafood market lunch, a towering transforming mecha statue, and one of the finest art-supply stores on the planet. The route forms a natural geographic arc — Toyosu → Tsukiji → Odaiba → Shinagawa — minimising backtracking while hitting each stop at its ideal time. Expect a leisurely but full day, with plenty of breathing room at every stop.

## Itinerary

🗺️ [Get transit directions from your accommodation to teamLab Planets TOKYO DMM](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=teamLab+Planets+TOKYO+DMM,+6-1-16+Toyosu,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Morning — teamLab Planets TOKYO DMM (08:30)

[![teamLab Planets TOKYO — immersive water and flower art](https://assets.team-lab.com/b5EBo9Uo-OK6SM09ZTkEZQ/89JNH3JuCgHZcGbACeD2vU/width=1200,format=jpeg)](https://www.teamlab.art/e/planets/ "teamLab Planets TOKYO — immersive water and flower art")

**📍 6-1-16 Toyosu, Koto-ku, Tokyo**  
**Admission:** ¥3,200–¥3,800/adult (dynamic pricing; buy online in advance)  
**Website:** https://www.teamlab.art/e/planets/

Start the day as soon as the doors open at the world's most visited digital art museum. teamLab Planets is a body-immersive experience where you walk **barefoot through shallow water**, pass through towering fields of floating flowers, and dissolve into infinite mirror rooms — all artworks respond dynamically to your presence. Unlike teamLab Borderless, everything here is sequential and intimate: visitors move through a curated path of four large water-based art spaces and two outdoor garden areas. Plan for around **2 hours** — early morning slots are quieter and feel more contemplative. Budget ¥3,200–¥3,800 for the ticket.

- **Book online in advance** — timed-entry tickets sell out, especially on weekends
- Remove shoes and socks and stow them in provided bags before entering the water zones; bring a small dry bag for valuables
- Wear clothing you don't mind getting slightly damp — the water is shallow but splashing happens
- Download the **teamLab app** before visiting to enhance the experience

🗺️ [Directions to Tsukiji Outer Market (Toyosu Station → Tsukijishijo Station, ~15 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=teamLab+Planets+TOKYO+DMM,+6-1-16+Toyosu,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&destination=Tsukiji+Outer+Market,+4-16-2+Tsukiji,+Chuo-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

> Take the **Yurikamome Line** one stop west to Shimbashi, then the **Tokyo Metro Hibiya Line** to Tsukijishijo Station (3-min walk to market), or walk/cycle the 2.5 km along the waterfront. Total: ~15–20 min by transit.

### Late Morning / Lunch — Tsukiji Outer Market (11:00–13:00)

[![Tsukiji Outer Market — Tokyo's legendary seafood and street food lanes](https://www.tsukiji.or.jp/assets/images/ogp.jpg?210212)](https://www.tsukiji.or.jp/english/ "Tsukiji Outer Market — Tokyo's legendary seafood and street food lanes")

**📍 4-16-2 Tsukiji, Chuo-ku, Tokyo 104-0045**  
**Admission:** Free (pay per purchase)  
**Website:** https://www.tsukiji.or.jp/english/

Arriving around 11:00 hits the sweet spot: the morning crowds have thinned, but almost all stalls are still open and stocked (many begin to close by 13:00–14:00). Tsukiji Outer Market is a labyrinth of narrow alleys lined with hundreds of small shops and eateries — wander freely and graze your way through lunch. Allow **1.5–2 hours**.

**What to eat and buy:**
- **Fresh sushi** — standing sushi bars dot the alleys; look for seasonal omakase sets (¥1,500–¥3,000)
- **Tamagoyaki** — thick, sweet rolled egg omelette served on a stick; several vendors offer free samples
- **Uni (sea urchin) on rice** — a Tsukiji signature; expect ¥1,500–¥2,500 for a bowl
- **Japanese kitchen knives** — world-class blades at professional prices; many shops will engrave handles
- **Dried goods and bonito flakes** — great edible souvenirs

Budget ¥2,000–¥4,000 for a full Tsukiji lunch crawl.

> ⚠️ Most vendors start packing up by 13:00 — make sure to finish eating and shopping by then to be comfortable.

🗺️ [Directions to RG 1/1 RX-0 Unicorn Gundam, Odaiba (Tsukijishijo → Shimbashi → Yurikamome to Daiba, ~35 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Tsukiji+Outer+Market,+4-16-2+Tsukiji,+Chuo-ku,+Tokyo&destination=DiverCity+Tokyo+Plaza,+1-1-10+Aomi,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

> Head to **Tsukijishijo Station**, take the Hibiya Line one stop to Shimbashi, then the **Yurikamome automated monorail** to **Daiba Station** (~25 min, scenic bay crossing). DiverCity Tokyo Plaza is a 5-min walk from the station.

### Afternoon — RG 1/1 RX-0 Unicorn Gundam, Odaiba (13:30)

[![DiverCity Tokyo Plaza and Unicorn Gundam statue, Odaiba](https://mitsui-shopping-park.com/divercity-tokyo/common/image/common/og-divercity-tokyo.jpg)](https://mitsui-shopping-park.com/divercity-tokyo/ "DiverCity Tokyo Plaza and Unicorn Gundam statue, Odaiba")

**📍 DiverCity Tokyo Plaza, 1-1-10 Aomi, Koto-ku, Tokyo 135-0064**  
**Admission:** Free (statue & plaza)  
**Website:** https://mitsui-shopping-park.com/divercity-tokyo/

Standing 19.7 metres tall outside DiverCity Tokyo Plaza, the life-size **RX-0 Unicorn Gundam** is Tokyo's most spectacular piece of public mecha art. The statue **physically transforms** between Unicorn Mode and Destroy Mode during scheduled daily shows — lights flood the frame, mechanical sounds boom across the plaza, and the psycho-frame glows green. Shows run approximately every two hours (check the DiverCity website for the day's schedule). Allow **1.5 hours** here.

- Grab a spot in front of the statue 5–10 minutes before a show for the best view
- Head up to the 7th floor of DiverCity for **THE GUNDAM BASE TOKYO** — a massive Gundam merchandise shop selling exclusive model kits, dioramas, and collectibles
- The free **Odaiba Marine Park** (5-min walk south) has unbeatable views of Rainbow Bridge and the Tokyo skyline — a peaceful post-Gundam stroll

🗺️ [Directions to PIGMENT TOKYO, Shinagawa (Daiba → Tennozu Isle, ~30 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=DiverCity+Tokyo+Plaza,+1-1-10+Aomi,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&destination=PIGMENT+TOKYO,+2-5-5+Higashi-Shinagawa,+Shinagawa-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

> Take the **Yurikamome Line** back to Shimbashi, transfer to the **JR Yamanote or Keihin-Tohoku Line** to Shinagawa, then the **Tokyo Monorail** or a short walk to **Tennozu Isle Station**. PIGMENT TOKYO is a 3-min walk from the station. Total: ~30 min.

### Late Afternoon — PIGMENT TOKYO (15:30)

[![PIGMENT TOKYO — world-class art materials store at Tennozu Isle](https://pigment.tokyo/cdn/shop/files/993758870d46726c8328aa9c2dd7c1df.jpg?v=1691636881)](https://pigment.tokyo/en "PIGMENT TOKYO — world-class art materials store at Tennozu Isle")

**📍 TERRADA Harbor One Building 1F, 2-5-5 Higashi-Shinagawa, Shinagawa-ku, Tokyo 140-0002**  
**Admission:** Free to browse  
**Opening hours:** 11:00–19:00 (closed Mondays and Tuesdays)  
**Website:** https://pigment.tokyo/en

The final stop is a revelation for anyone interested in art, craft, or colour. PIGMENT TOKYO stocks an extraordinary **~4,500 pigment colours** — including rare traditional Japanese mineral pigments (iwa-enogu), historical sumi ink sticks, gold and silver leaf, and cutting-edge metallic pigments — plus over **600 brush varieties** from around the world. Located in the quietly elegant Tennozu Isle arts district, it's operated by Warehouse TERRADA and staffed by practising artists who offer workshops and project consultations. Allow **45–60 minutes** to browse; it's impossible to rush.

- Even if you're not buying, the pigment displays are visually stunning — hundreds of glowing mineral jars arranged by colour and origin
- The adjacent **TERRADA Art Complex** and warehouse arts buildings are worth a quick stroll for gallery-hopping
- Budget ¥1,000–¥5,000+ if you plan to purchase pigments, papers, or brushes

## Tips

- **Book teamLab Planets tickets online well in advance** — timed-entry tickets sell out days or weeks ahead, especially on weekends and public holidays
- **Visit Tsukiji between 11:00 and 13:00** — early enough for fresh stock and a relaxed pace, but late enough to avoid peak morning crush; most stalls begin closing after 13:00
- Get a **Suica or Pasmo IC card** at any major station for seamless transit across all four stops (trains, Yurikamome monorail, and Tokyo Monorail all accept IC cards)
- **PIGMENT TOKYO is closed on Mondays and Tuesdays** — plan accordingly; it also closes for occasional workshops so check their calendar at pigment.tokyo
- The Gundam **transformation show schedule** varies by day — check the DiverCity website the morning of your visit and time your arrival to catch one

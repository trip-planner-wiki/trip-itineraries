# Shibuya Sky, Harajuku & Memory Lane

**City:** Tokyo
**Best for:** sightseeing, views, fashion, shopping, food, nightlife
**Approximate duration:** Full day (10–11 hours)
**Estimated cost:** ¥5,000–¥15,000 (¥3,000 Shibuya Sky + food + shopping)

---

## Overview

This curated day trip takes you through the beating heart of Tokyo's youth and pop culture. Start with the world-famous Shibuya Scramble Crossing before heading up to Shibuya Sky for sweeping 360° views over the metropolis. From there, a short hop brings you to Harajuku's iconic Takeshita Street for kawaii fashion and crepes, then back to Shibuya's Loft for design gifts and souvenirs. The evening caps off with yakitori and cold beer in the nostalgic narrow alleys of Shinjuku's Memory Lane (Omoide Yokocho) — a Showa-era izakaya alley that has barely changed in decades.

## Itinerary

### Morning — Shibuya Crossing (渋谷スクランブル交差点) (09:30)

[![Shibuya Scramble Crossing, Tokyo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/1/17/Shibuya_Crossing_20241020.jpg/960px-Shibuya_Crossing_20241020.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shibuya_Crossing_20241020.jpg "Shibuya Scramble Crossing, Tokyo")

**📍 In front of Shibuya Station Hachikō Exit, Shibuya-ku**

Step out of Shibuya Station's Hachikō Exit and you are standing at the world's busiest pedestrian scramble crossing, where thousands of people flow from all directions simultaneously when the lights turn. It is free and open 24/7, but a weekday morning around 09:30–10:00 is perfect — busy enough to be atmospheric without the weekend crush. Pop up to the Starbucks on the second floor of the Tsutaya building across the crossing for a coffee and a bird's-eye view before heading to your next stop. Free entry; budget ¥600–¥800 for coffee.

🗺️ [Directions to Shibuya Sky (walk, ~5 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Shibuya+Scramble+Crossing,+Shibuya-ku,+Tokyo&destination=Shibuya+Sky,+2-24-12+Shibuya,+Shibuya-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Mid-Morning — Shibuya Sky (渋谷スカイ) (10:00)

[![Shibuya Sky outdoor observation deck, Tokyo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/a/a1/Shibuya_Sky_Observation_Deck_%2853415730632%29.jpg/960px-Shibuya_Sky_Observation_Deck_%2853415730632%29.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shibuya_Sky_Observation_Deck_(53415730632).jpg "Shibuya Sky outdoor observation deck, Shibuya Scramble Square, Tokyo")

**📍 Shibuya Scramble Square 14F/45F/46F, 2-24-12 Shibuya, Shibuya-ku**

Ride the elevator up to the outdoor rooftop observation deck atop Shibuya Scramble Square — at 229 m above street level this is one of the best vantage points in Tokyo. Arrive right at opening (10:00) to enjoy the panorama before crowds build. The open-air rooftop wraps 360° around the top of the building with views across to Mt Fuji on clear days, and straight down to the crossing below. Advance booking online is strongly recommended; walk-in tickets are available but can sell out. Admission ¥3,000; budget ~1 hour.

🗺️ [Directions to Harajuku (JR Yamanote Line, ~2 min or walk ~15 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Shibuya+Scramble+Square,+2-24-12+Shibuya,+Shibuya-ku,+Tokyo&destination=Takeshita+Street,+Harajuku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Late Morning — Harajuku (原宿) — Takeshita Street (11:15)

[![Takeshita Street, Harajuku, Tokyo — kawaii fashion boutiques](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/d/dd/Harajuku_-_Takeshita_Street_13_%2815120530713%29.jpg/960px-Harajuku_-_Takeshita_Street_13_%2815120530713%29.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Harajuku_-_Takeshita_Street_13_(15120530713).jpg "Takeshita Street, Harajuku, Tokyo — kawaii fashion boutiques and crepe stands")

**📍 Near Harajuku Station (JR Yamanote Line), Jingumae, Shibuya-ku**

Harajuku's 350-metre Takeshita Street is the spiritual home of Tokyo's kawaii subculture — crammed with pastel-coloured boutiques, vintage shops, accessory stalls, and legendary crepe stands. Browse the fashion, pick up a rainbow crepe or a cotton candy cloud, and soak in the colour. Weekday mornings are calmer than weekends. Free to browse; crepe ¥600–¥900, shopping budget varies.

🗺️ [Directions to Shibuya Loft (walk, ~15 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Takeshita+Street,+Harajuku,+Tokyo&destination=Shibuya+Loft,+21-1+Udagawacho,+Shibuya-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Early Afternoon — Shibuya Loft (渋谷ロフト) (13:00)

[![Loft lifestyle store exterior, Tokyo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/d/d2/Loft_kichijoji.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Loft_kichijoji.jpg "Loft lifestyle design store exterior, Tokyo")

**📍 21-1 Udagawacho, Shibuya-ku (open 11:00–21:00)**

Head back into Shibuya and spend an hour or two in Loft — Japan's best lifestyle and design store, spread across seven floors. Loft is the ideal place for thoughtful souvenirs and gifts: high-quality stationery, clever kitchen gadgets, travel accessories, seasonal items, and uniquely Japanese design goods not easily found elsewhere. Free entry; budget ¥1,000–¥5,000 depending on willpower. Grab lunch nearby before or after — Udagawacho has plenty of affordable cafes and ramen shops.

🗺️ [Directions to Memory Lane / Omoide Yokocho (JR Yamanote Line, ~15 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Shibuya+Loft,+21-1+Udagawacho,+Shibuya-ku,+Tokyo&destination=Omoide+Yokocho,+1-2-11+Nishi-Shinjuku,+Shinjuku-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Evening — Omoide Yokocho / Memory Lane (思い出横丁) (17:30)

[![Omoide Yokocho Memory Lane izakaya alley, Shinjuku, Tokyo](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/9/9d/Shinjuku-West_Omoide-Yokocho.jpg/960px-Shinjuku-West_Omoide-Yokocho.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Shinjuku-West_Omoide-Yokocho.jpg "Omoide Yokocho (Memory Lane), Shinjuku, Tokyo — narrow izakaya alley")

**📍 1-2-11 Nishi-Shinjuku, Shinjuku-ku (near Shinjuku Station west exit)**

Take the Yamanote Line one stop to Shinjuku and walk to Memory Lane — a narrow alley of tiny izakayas that has changed little since the post-war Showa era. Smoke curls from skewers of yakitori charring over charcoal, red lanterns glow, and bar owners call out greetings from counters barely wide enough for five people. Stalls open from around 17:00 and the alley fills up fast, so arrive early. Pick any stall that catches your eye and squeeze in for a beer and a plate of yakitori. Budget ¥2,000–¥4,000 per person for drinks and food.

## Tips

- Book **Shibuya Sky tickets online in advance** at [shibuya-scramble-square.com/sky/](https://www.shibuya-scramble-square.com/sky/) — same-day walk-in tickets are available but often sell out, especially on weekends.
- The **observation deck opens at 10:00** sharp; arriving right at opening gives you the clearest air and smallest crowds before the tourist rush.
- Harajuku's **Takeshita Street is far calmer on weekdays** — weekend afternoons can feel overwhelming with the crowds.
- **Omoide Yokocho fills up quickly after 18:00** — arrive by 17:30 to get a seat comfortably without queueing.
- **IC cards** (Suica, Pasmo) work on all JR and Tokyo Metro trains connecting these stops; bring some cash for izakaya drinks and food.

## Notes

- Shibuya Sky requires **advance ticket booking** — book online before your visit
- Memory Lane izakayas are **cash-preferred**; bring ¥3,000–¥5,000 per person for drinks and food
- teamLab Planets requires **advance online ticket purchase** — timed-entry slots sell out; book before arrival
- **PIGMENT TOKYO is closed Mondays and Tuesdays** — check the calendar before scheduling the Art, Anime & Markets trip
- **Shinjuku Gyoen is closed Mondays** — avoid scheduling the Shinjuku day trip on a Monday
- **Omoide Yokocho stalls are cash only** — bring ¥3,000–¥4,000 per person in cash before entering the alley

## Overview

[![Desho Fes!! 2026 — official event visual](https://ticket.tv-asahi.co.jp/resources/pv_20260205044135_621b46fe_249ba360.jpg)](https://ticket.tv-asahi.co.jp/ex/project/desho_fes2026)

The 5th anniversary celebration concert of the popular variety TV show "Dou ka shiteru ze!" co-hosted by anime song singer Oishi Masayoshi (OxT — known for the Overlord and SSSS.Gridman opening themes) and idol/singer Suzuki Airi. Also featuring anime singer TRUE (Karasawa Miho — Is It Wrong to Pick Up Girls in a Dungeon?, Yuki Yuna is a Hero) and こっちのけんと. A smaller, more intimate fan event compared to stadium concerts — an excellent evening out for fans of anime music.

> **Date-specific:** Evening concert on May 20, 2026.

The Tokyo Port Festival (Minato Matsuri) commemorates the opening of the Port of Tokyo to international trade on May 20, 1941. Organizations associated with the port host special events: guided tours and rides on specialized vessels (fireboats, patrol ships, container ships), maritime exhibitions, and waterfront activities. Free admission makes this one of the best value festival days in Tokyo. The waterfront atmosphere and unique vessel access make it a memorable, relaxed day out.

> **Date-specific:** This festival occurs on May 20 every year to mark the anniversary.

## Itinerary

🗺️ [Get transit directions from your accommodation to Tokyo Port Odaiba Waterfront](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&destination=Odaiba+Waterfront,+Odaiba,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Morning — Tokyo Port Festival Vessels & Exhibitions (10:00)
**📍 Tokyo Port Odaiba Waterfront Area**

Arrive early to join guided vessel tours before queues grow. Fireboats, patrol ships, and container ships are opened to the public — a unique chance to see maritime Tokyo up close. The maritime exhibition showcases the history and operation of Tokyo Port, one of the busiest ports in East Asia.

🗺️ [Directions to Odaiba dining area (walk, ~10 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Odaiba+Waterfront,+Odaiba,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&destination=DiverCity+Tokyo+Plaza,+Odaiba,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Lunch — Odaiba Dining (12:30)
Odaiba has a wide range of dining options from food courts to waterfront restaurants. Decks Tokyo Beach and DiverCity Tokyo Plaza both have excellent food halls. Budget ¥1,000–¥2,000.

🗺️ [Directions to DiverCity / Unicorn Gundam (walk, ~5 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Odaiba+Waterfront,+Odaiba,+Koto-ku,+Tokyo&destination=DiverCity+Tokyo+Plaza,+Odaiba,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Afternoon — Odaiba Waterfront & Unicorn Gundam (14:00)
After the festival activities, explore Odaiba at leisure. The life-size Unicorn Gundam statue at DiverCity Tokyo Plaza is a short walk away. Odaiba Marine Park beach offers beautiful views of Rainbow Bridge. This is also a great time to visit teamLab Planets (if booked in advance) or simply relax by the water.

🗺️ [Directions to Tokyo Dome / Suidobashi for Nogizaka46 Day 2 (transit, ~40 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=DiverCity+Tokyo+Plaza,+Odaiba,+Tokyo&destination=Tokyo+Dome,+1-3-61+Koraku,+Bunkyo-ku,+Tokyo&travelmode=transit)

### Evening — Nogizaka46 Birthday Live Day 2 (18:30, optional)
If you have tickets, May 20 is Day 2 of the Nogizaka46 14th Birthday Live concert at Tokyo Dome. You can combine the morning festival with the evening concert for a full-day experience — travel from Odaiba to Suidobashi takes about 40 minutes. (See the Nogizaka46 day-trip page for concert details.)

## Tips
- Check the official Tokyo Port Festival website closer to the date for the specific vessel lineup and boarding locations
- The festival is free, but vessel tours may have limited capacity — arrive early
- Odaiba is easily reached via the Yurikamome monorail from Shimbashi (20 min)

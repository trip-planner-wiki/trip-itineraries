# Tokyo: Day-Trip Itineraries

**Stay dates:** May 18–23, 2026  
**City overview:** Tokyo is Japan's dynamic capital — a vast metropolis blending ultra-modern technology culture, traditional neighborhoods, and world-class dining. The otaku districts of Akihabara and Nakano, the fashion hubs of Shibuya and Harajuku, the luxurious Ginza shopping streets, and the futuristic Odaiba waterfront all offer distinctly different day-trip experiences.

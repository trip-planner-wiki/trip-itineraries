# Uji Day Trip: Byodo-in & Tea Culture

**City:** Kyoto (day trip to Uji)  
**Best for:** Historic sites, Japanese culture, matcha, relaxing  
**Approximate duration:** Full day (6–7 hours)  
**Estimated cost:** ¥2,000–¥5,000

---

## Overview

Uji is a small city just 15 km south of central Kyoto, famous for two things: the magnificent Byodo-in temple complex and Japan's finest matcha (green tea). This makes for a deeply satisfying half or full day trip — visiting one of Japan's most iconic UNESCO World Heritage buildings, then indulging in the region's matcha culture at traditional teahouses along the riverside. The Phoenix Hall (Hou-ou-do), built in 1053, was designed to evoke the paradise of Amida Buddha and is so iconic that its image appears on the Japanese 10-yen coin. Uji's matcha cultivation dates back to the 13th century when the monk Myoe brought tea plants from China; today the city produces some of the highest-grade ceremonial matcha in Japan, and its riverside teahouses offer an authentic taste of this centuries-old tradition.

### Late Morning — Uji Riverside & Teahouses (10:00)

[![Uji River, Kyoto Prefecture — riverside teahouses](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ec/Uji_river%2C_Kyoto.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Uji_river,_Kyoto.jpg "Uji River, Kyoto Prefecture")

Walk from Uji Station along the Uji River. The entire main street leading to Byodo-in is lined with matcha teahouses and shops. Most offer samples — try:
- Matcha soft serve ice cream (¥400–¥600)
- Matcha parfait (¥800–¥1,200)
- Tsuyu-kiri (cold matcha jelly dessert) in season

🗺️ [Directions to Byodo-in (walk, ~5 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Uji+River,+Uji+City,+Kyoto+Prefecture&destination=Byodo-in,+116+Renge,+Uji+City,+Kyoto+Prefecture&travelmode=transit)

### Byodo-in (11:00)

[![Byodo-in Phoenix Hall, Uji](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/5/59/Byodoin_Phoenix_Hall_Uji_2009.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Byodoin_Phoenix_Hall_Uji_2009.jpg "Byodo-in Phoenix Hall, Uji")

**📍 116 Renge, Uji City, Kyoto Prefecture**
**Entry:** Adults ¥600 (garden + Phoenix Hall ¥1,000)**

Originally a Fujiwara clan villa (built 998), converted into a Buddhist temple in 1052. The Phoenix Hall (Hou-ou-do), built in 1053, is one of Japan's finest surviving Heian-period architectural masterpieces — a National Treasure and UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is featured on the Japanese 10-yen coin. The reflection in the pond is breathtaking on calm mornings. The Phoenix Hall interior can be visited on guided tours (entry reservation at the site). Allow 1.5–2 hours.

### Lunch — Uji Riverside (13:00)

[![Uji River, Kyoto Prefecture — riverside dining](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ec/Uji_river%2C_Kyoto.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Uji_river,_Kyoto.jpg "Uji riverside restaurants, Kyoto")

Several excellent restaurants and teahouses serve lunch along the riverside. Try a set meal featuring Uji tea-flavored noodles (cha-soba) or a kaiseki-style lunch. Budget ¥1,000–¥2,500.

🗺️ [Directions to Ujigami Shrine (walk, ~10 min)](https://www.google.com/maps/dir/?api=1&origin=Byodo-in,+116+Renge,+Uji+City,+Kyoto+Prefecture&destination=Ujigami+Shrine,+Uji+City,+Kyoto+Prefecture&travelmode=transit)

### Afternoon — Uji Shrine & Town Walk (14:30)

[![Uji Shrine, Uji City, Kyoto](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/0/05/Haiden_or_Kiriharaden%2C_Uji_shrine_-_Mar_1%2C_2008.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Haiden_or_Kiriharaden,_Uji_shrine_-_Mar_1,_2008.jpg "Uji Shrine, Uji City, Kyoto")

Explore Ujigami Shrine — the oldest surviving shrine building in Japan (late Heian period, ~10th–11th century), also a UNESCO World Heritage Site and free to enter. Walk the pleasant riverside paths back to the station.

### Return to Kyoto (16:00)

[![Uji River, Kyoto Prefecture](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/e/ec/Uji_river%2C_Kyoto.jpg)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Uji_river,_Kyoto.jpg "Uji River, Kyoto Prefecture — return journey")

Return JR Nara Line from Uji to Kyoto Station (~17 minutes).

## Tips
- Morning light makes Byodo-in's pond reflection most spectacular
- The Phoenix Hall interior guided tours (additional ¥300) are worth booking — they run in timed slots
- Uji's matcha is recognized as the highest grade in Japan — buy some to take home
- Combine with a visit to Fujinomori Shrine (Sumizome Station, one stop north) for the Ajisai Matsuri hydrangea season from June 1

# Uji Day Trip: Byodo-in & Tea Culture

**City:** Kyoto (day trip to Uji)  
**Best for:** Historic sites, Japanese culture, matcha, relaxing  
**Approximate duration:** Full day (6–7 hours)  
**Estimated cost:** ¥2,000–¥5,000

---

## Overview

Uji is a small city just 15 km south of central Kyoto, famous for two things: the magnificent Byodo-in temple complex and Japan's finest matcha (green tea). This makes for a deeply satisfying half or full day trip — visiting one of Japan's most iconic UNESCO World Heritage buildings, then indulging in the region's matcha culture at traditional teahouses along the riverside.

---

## Itinerary

### Morning — Travel to Uji (09:30)
**📍 Uji City, Kyoto Prefecture**

From Kyoto Station, take the JR Nara Line directly to Uji Station (~17 minutes, ¥240). Alternatively, Kintetsu Kintetsu-Uji Station is a short walk from the main sights.

### Late Morning — Uji Riverside & Teahouses (10:00)
Walk from Uji Station along the Uji River. The entire main street leading to Byodo-in is lined with matcha teahouses and shops. Most offer samples — try:
- Matcha soft serve ice cream (¥400–¥600)
- Matcha parfait (¥800–¥1,200)
- Tsuyu-kiri (cold matcha jelly dessert) in season

### Byodo-in (11:00)
**📍 116 Renge, Uji City, Kyoto Prefecture**
**Entry:** Adults ¥600 (garden + Phoenix Hall ¥1,000)**

Originally a Fujiwara clan villa (built 998), converted into a Buddhist temple in 1052. The Phoenix Hall (Hou-ou-do), built in 1053, is one of Japan's finest surviving Heian-period architectural masterpieces — a National Treasure and UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is featured on the Japanese 10-yen coin. The reflection in the pond is breathtaking on calm mornings. The Phoenix Hall interior can be visited on guided tours (entry reservation at the site). Allow 1.5–2 hours.

### Lunch — Uji Riverside (13:00)
Several excellent restaurants and teahouses serve lunch along the riverside. Try a set meal featuring Uji tea-flavored noodles (cha-soba) or a kaiseki-style lunch. Budget ¥1,000–¥2,500.

### Afternoon — Uji Shrine & Town Walk (14:30)
Explore Ujigami Shrine — the oldest surviving shrine building in Japan (late Heian period, ~10th–11th century), also a UNESCO World Heritage Site and free to enter. Walk the pleasant riverside paths back to the station.

### Return to Kyoto (16:00)
Return JR Nara Line from Uji to Kyoto Station (~17 minutes).

---

## Tips
- Morning light makes Byodo-in's pond reflection most spectacular
- The Phoenix Hall interior guided tours (additional ¥300) are worth booking — they run in timed slots
- Uji's matcha is recognized as the highest grade in Japan — buy some to take home
- Combine with a visit to Fujinomori Shrine (Sumizome Station, one stop north) for the Ajisai Matsuri hydrangea season from June 1

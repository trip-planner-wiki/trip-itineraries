# Kyoto: Day-Trip Itineraries

**Stay dates:** May 31 – June 6, 2026  
**City overview:** Kyoto is Japan's cultural heart — the ancient imperial capital, home to over 1,600 Buddhist temples, 400 Shinto shrines, and 17 UNESCO World Heritage Sites. In early June, the city transitions from spring to early summer: vivid green foliage, the opening of hydrangea season, and some of the most atmospheric traditional festivals in Japan. Beyond the temples, Kyoto also has a thriving food culture, excellent shopping, and a surprisingly active maid cafe scene.

## Notes
- **June 1 is extremely full** — Kifune Matsuri (daytime), Kyoto Takigi Nō (evening), Ajisai Matsuri (morning), and the Antique Fair all start simultaneously. Prioritize by interest.
- **Suggested June 1 combination:** Morning at Fujinomori hydrangeas → Afternoon at Kifune Matsuri → Evening at Kyoto Takigi Nō
- The Gintama × PUI PUI Molcar popup (from May 30, Osaka) continues through June 7 — a day trip back to Osaka is possible if you missed it
- Kurama Onsen is excellent for a wellness/relaxation day during the Kyoto stay

## Trip Overview

This is a 20-day Japan trip covering three major cities, each with a distinct character:

- **Tokyo** — Japan's modern megalopolis: otaku culture, digital art, idol concerts, and world-class dining
- **Osaka** — Japan's food capital: street food, craft beer, Universal Studios, and Den-Den Town
- **Kyoto** — Japan's cultural heart: ancient temples, traditional festivals, onsen, and mountain shrines

Each city directory contains:
- **General day-trip itineraries** — flexible trips that can be done on any day during the stay
- **Date-specific event pages** — events and festivals tied to specific dates, named with date prefixes (e.g., `2026-05-20-event-name.md`)

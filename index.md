## How to Use This Wiki

Each city directory contains:
1. An **`index.md`** listing all day trips with brief descriptions
2. **General day-trip pages** — flexible options for any day of the stay
3. **Date-specific event pages** — prefixed with the date (e.g., `2026-05-20-...`) for events tied to specific days

Browse the city index to choose day trips based on your mood, interests, and available dates.
